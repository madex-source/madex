Landscape Development

‌The Shavdaahgriham

And the Masterplan


Ranighat, Birgunj 2021 A.D.


The design of the park with a crematorium integrates modern aesthetics with elements that promote tranquility and respect. The landscaping throughout the park includes carefully selected plant species that thrive in the local terai climate, providing year-round beauty with seasonal flowers, evergreen trees, and manicured lawns. Water features are strategically placed, incorporating nature to enhance the peaceful atmosphere.


The scattering gardens are designed with careful attention to privacy and serenity. Sustainable architecture is a key focus.

Native plantings and eco-friendly materials are used throughout, ensuring that the park is not only beautiful but also environmentally responsible.

The design aims to create a harmonious and respectful environment that honors the memory of loved ones while providing a peaceful retreat for visitors.

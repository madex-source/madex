From initial topographical survey and conceptual masterplan to 4K 3D visualizations, seismic engineering blueprints, and turnkey construction supervision.

01

Topographical Map

Precision total-station land survey, contour mapping, boundary demarcation, and digital elevation profiling.

02

Overall Masterplan

Holistic site planning, zoning regulations, solar orientation, vehicular circulation, and landscape integration.

03

Detailed Architectural Plan

Bespoke floor plans, sections, elevations, working drawings, and municipality approval documentation.

04

3D Walkthrough Video

Photorealistic 4K exterior/interior rendering, cinematic daylight walkthrough animations, and virtual reality tours.

05

Structural Details

Seismic resistant structural analysis (ETABS/SAP), ductile reinforcement detailing, and foundation design.

06

Electrical Details

Complete load calculation, conduit schematics, DB scheduling, backup generator sizing, and smart home automation.

07

Sanitary Details

Water supply schematics, storm drainage design, septic tank sizing, solar water piping, and fixture schedules.

08

Government DUDBC and market-rate itemized cost estimation covering civil, finishing, and MEP works.

09

Bill of Quantities (BOQ)

Procurement Consult & Supervision

Turnkey site supervision, reinforcement checking before casting, material testing, and quality control.

Project Metadata

Redevelopment of Ranighat Samshanghat

of Birgunj Metropolitan City Office • 2021 A.D.

• Form Development

"The space enclosed by a piece of cloth covering is very often used as a place for kriya karma. The basic form of tent inspired the overall form development. Over ornamentation and decorativeness would rather amplify anger. Minimalist approach in form creates a calming environment."

• Vernacular Materiality

"Use of Traditional Khapada in the roof is an approach for preservation of heritage carried by the indigenous people of Birgunj."

• Landscaping & Scattering Gardens

"The landscaping throughout the park includes carefully selected plant species that thrive in the local Terai climate, providing year-round beauty with seasonal flowers, evergreen trees, and manicured lawns. The scattering gardens are designed with careful attention to privacy and serenity."

About Scale and Spaces_

Scale and Spaces Private Limited is a comprehensive architecture, structural engineering, interior design, and construction management firm operating actively with dual offices in Tokha-6, Kathmandu and Birgunj-12, Parsa.

Our firm was established to bridge the gap between creative architectural aesthetics and rigorous structural integrity. We treat every scale of design — from regional masterplans, hospitals, and civic crematoriums to bespoke residences and refined minimalist interiors — with uncompromised attention to contextual materiality, climate comfort, and client aspirations.

With deep roots across both the Kathmandu Valley and the Terai region, our multidisciplinary team provides turnkey architectural planning, 3D walkthrough visualization, structural blueprints under Nepal Building Code (NBC), municipal approval clearances, and daily on-site supervision.

Our Core Principles
  • •
    Contextual & Climate-Responsive Architecture Designing with local microclimates in mind — mitigating intense Terai heat through deep overhangs, cross-ventilation, and louvers, while maximizing southern mountain winter warmth in the Kathmandu Valley.
  • •
    Seismic & Structural Safety First In-house structural engineering ensuring every residential, commercial, or institutional structure exceeds NBC seismic standards and ductile reinforcement criteria.
  • •
    Heritage Preservation with Modernism As demonstrated in the Ranighat Crematorium, we weave indigenous materials like traditional Khapada tiles and vernacular forms into contemporary minimalist spaces.
  • •
    Turnkey Transparency & BOQ Precision Every project is backed by comprehensive Bill of Quantities (BOQ), rate analysis, and strict procurement checks to guarantee on-budget completion.

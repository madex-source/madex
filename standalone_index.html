FAQS

Common questions on architectural workflow, Nepal NBC structural design, timelines & supervision.

Ask a Specific Question →
We deliver complete end-to-end services under one roof: topographical site survey, masterplanning, conceptual & municipal architectural plans, photorealistic 3D views/walkthroughs, Nepal National Building Code (NBC) seismic structural design, electrical & sanitary MEP drawings, detailed cost estimation & BOQ, and construction site supervision.
Yes. Every building design is engineered against the latest Nepal National Building Code (NBC) seismic provisions and tailored to local Metropolitan or Rural Municipality setbacks, FAR, ground coverage, and structural check documentation across Kathmandu Valley and Terai cities.
For standard residences, initial spatial concepts take 5 to 7 working days after site measurement. Once layouts are finalized, photorealistic 3D views and integrated architectural, structural, electrical, and sanitary permit/construction sets typically complete within 2 to 4 weeks depending on scale.
Yes. We prepare photorealistic exterior perspectives, lighting mood renders, material boards, and 3D walkthrough studies so clients can finalize facades, tile/timber palettes, ceiling coves, and wardrobe layouts before contractor execution begins.
Our itemized quantity takeoff covers excavation, reinforcement steel, concrete grades, brickwork, finishes, and MEP fixtures prior to tendering. This enables transparent contractor comparison and minimizes mid-project scope surprises.
